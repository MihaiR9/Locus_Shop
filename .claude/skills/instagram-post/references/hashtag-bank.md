# Hashtag Bank — Finbot Instagram

Curated set of Romanian-targeted, fintech / antreprenor hashtags.
Pick 10-15 per post from this bank. Don't invent new ones; rotate
within the bank to match the pillar.

The bank is organised by purpose. A healthy post pulls from at least
three of the four buckets.

## Tags to avoid

- Generic English (#money, #business, #entrepreneur) — too broad,
  zero local relevance, signals "spam" to the IG algorithm.
- Hashtag stuffing past 20 tags. IG explicitly downweights it now.
- Niche memes (#tvalife, #anafstories) that don't have an audience.
- Anything with capitalised acronyms in unusual mixes (#TVA_2026 etc.) —
  hashtags should look natural.
- Competitor brand tags (#saga, #winmentor). Petty and counter-positioned.
