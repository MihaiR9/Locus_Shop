# Post Archetypes — Finbot Teaser Campaign

A teaser campaign needs rhythm. If every post is a "Big Statement", the
feed becomes flat. The three variants below are the building blocks; the
archetypes are recipes that combine them.

The goal of every post is the same: **make the antreprenor curious enough
to enter their email on the wishlist**. Curiosity comes from contrast —
a number that feels too high, a question that hits a daily pain, a
promise that sounds too clean. Lean into one of these per post.

---

## The three variants, mapped to message shape

| Message shape | Variant | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Promise / tagline / declarative one-liner | `statement` | Big type, lime accent on the load-bearing word. Hero of the brand. |
| Single big stat, time/money/count | `number` | Cifra eroică in mono — feels precise and trustworthy. |
| Question that creates a pause | `question` | The opening quote frames it as a thought experiment, not a sales pitch. |

Default to `statement`. Use `number` when the cifra IS the post. Use
`question` no more than 1-in-4 — questions stop the scroll but they don't
sustain a feed.

## Archetypes (recipe + pillar + sample copy)

Each archetype below targets ONE pillar. Mix archetypes across the campaign
so the feed feels varied.

### A1 — The Promise (pillar: Cashflow / Taxe / Facturi / AI)
**Variant:** `statement` · **Theme:** `bone` or `ink`

Two-beat headline: setup + payoff. The payoff word goes in `*...*` for the
lime highlight.

- "Banii tăi. *Live.*" (Cashflow)
- "ANAF.\n*Fără stres.*" (Taxe)
- "Facturi. *Automat.*" (Facturi)
- "Un coleg care *nu doarme.*" (AI Assistant)

### A2 — The Cost of the Status Quo (pillar: depends on number)
**Variant:** `number` · **Theme:** `ink` or `moss`

Lead with a number that feels too high. Caption explains what it is.
Source line for credibility — even if it's "Estimare Finbot, 2026".

- `41h` lunar pe contabilitatea manuală. (Cashflow / Facturi)
- `73%` din SRL-urile mici nu știu cashflow-ul săptămânii. (Cashflow)
- `4` ore pe lună pe portalul ANAF. Pe minim. (Taxe)
- `12` aplicații deschise ca să vezi un cashflow. (Cashflow)

### A3 — The Provocation (pillar: any)
**Variant:** `question` · **Theme:** `moss` or `bone`

Question that an antreprenor has asked himself but nu îndrăznește să
spună cu voce tare. Don't answer it in the post — that's why he goes to
the wishlist.

- "Câte facturi ai în inbox și nu sunt în contabilitate?"
- "Când ai văzut ultima dată cashflow-ul real al firmei tale?"
- "Cât te costă o lună de TVA calculat manual?"
- "Câte aplicații deschizi ca să vezi cât ai în cont?"

### A4 — The Inversion (pillar: any)
**Variant:** `statement` · **Theme:** `lime` (use sparingly)

Take a complaint everyone has and invert it. Lime theme because it's loud
on purpose.

- "Contabilitatea. *Fără ședințe.*"
- "Facturi. *Fără Excel.*"
- "TVA. *Fără surprize.*"

### A5 — The Countdown (pillar: launch teaser)
**Variant:** `statement` · **Theme:** `ink`

Anchored to a date or a day count. Builds urgency in the final 2 weeks.

- "În *14 zile*, antreprenorii români nu mai pierd timp cu facturile."
- "*7 zile* până la lansare."
- "*Mâine.* La 09:00."

Use sparingly — only in the final stretch. Overusing kills the surprise.

### A6 — The Manifesto Line (pillar: brand-level)
**Variant:** `statement` · **Theme:** `bone` or `moss`

A line of belief, not a feature. For audience-building, not conversion.
Still ends with the wishlist CTA — the brand-level posts give the rest of
the campaign somewhere to live.

- "Antreprenorul nu e contabil. Nici nu trebuie să fie."
- "Cifra contează doar dacă o vezi la timp."
- "Decizia bună are nevoie de date proaspete."

## What NOT to make a post about

- Features in detail. We're teasing, not demoing.
- Comparisons against named competitors (e.g. "mai bun decât SAGA"). The
  posts feel petty and the audience is mixed.
- Internal milestones (closing pre-seed, hiring engineer #3). Not why
  the audience would sign up.
- Anything that needs more than ~12 words on the canvas. If you can't say
  it in 12 words, the post isn't ready — re-write the copy first.
